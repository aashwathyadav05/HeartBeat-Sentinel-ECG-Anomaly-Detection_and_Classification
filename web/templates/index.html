How it works and limitations

A shared 1D CNN feeds a reconstruction decoder and five independent classification outputs. Thresholds are selected on validation fold 9; test fold 10 is held out.

Anomaly scores measure reconstruction deviation and do not identify a condition. Class scores are not calibrated probabilities. Occlusion shows model sensitivity, not causal physiology.
